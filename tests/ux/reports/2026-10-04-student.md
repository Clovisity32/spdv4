## Measurements (after fixes)

- Load 230–260ms; adding a subject re-renders the plan in about 400ms.
- All 15 table boxes are labelled and at least 44px; no sideways page scroll at 390px; no page errors.
- Keyboard reaches the first box in 6 Tab presses.
- Tapping a box answers in about 60ms (your row) or about 335ms (a lower row).

## Fixed this session

1. **Shortest path.** "See where you can go: open Your Action Plan ↓" button under the subject list. It scrolls to the plan and moves focus to its heading.
2. **Clarity.** Lower-row details now show a short status line ("Still needs more work on this row: …") and the route on its own line ("To be on this row: Mother Tongue G3→G2 · Drop …"). "Closes nothing" became "closes no pathway".
3. **Feedback.** Details scroll to the top of the screen on tap (they could land off-screen on a phone). A one-line note explains the dashed "EST." boxes whenever they appear.

4. **Vague far-off boxes.** A ○ box on a lower row now names its marks ("+16 marks in 2 subjects"), filled in just after the first draw. Where marks cannot reach a pathway it says "Marks alone won't be enough".

## Not fixed (open)

- The plan still sits below the eligibility cards; the button works around it rather than moving it.
- A ○ box on your own row can still show a DROP suggestion that has nothing to do with marks.
- Wording still uses "Aggregate" and "courses"; not yet read-tested with a Sec 3 student.
