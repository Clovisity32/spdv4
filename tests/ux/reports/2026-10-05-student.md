## Measurements

- Entering six subjects: **25 interactions one at a time, 15 with the new panel** (about 4.9s vs 0.8s in the script, which only shows relative effort).
- Load about 200ms; the Action Plan appears about 400ms after the add click.
- All 15 table boxes are labelled and at least 44px; no sideways page scroll on desktop or phone; no page errors; keyboard reaches the first box in 6 Tab presses.
- Tapping a box answers in under 100ms (a lower row or your own row).

## Fixed this session

1. **Discoverability on phones (highest friction).** The new "Add several subjects at once" panel sat below six stacked fields, so a student had to scroll past them to find the faster route. A phone-only button at the top of the form, "Taking several subjects? Add them all at once ↓", now opens the panel and scrolls to it. It is hidden on wide screens, where the panel is already visible.
2. **Wrong popup text.** Adding a duplicate subject showed the explanation as the popup heading and "Duplicate Subject" as the text. Heading and text are now the right way round.

## Not fixed (open)

- The Action Plan is still 2,150px (desktop) and 3,400px (phone) down the page; the "See where you can go" button works around it.
- Requirement lines in "Why you qualify" and "Why not yet" are copied from the eligibility rules and read technically (for example "1 Relevant subject (G3) (R1 Category): …").
- "LDL does not increase eligibility" uses the school's term without spelling it out.
- Wording ("Aggregate", "courses") has not been read-tested with a real Sec 3 student.
